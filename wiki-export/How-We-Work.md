# 🔁 How We Work

## Team

Marieke (PO), Lotte (data science), Youssef (ML engineering). Tom (Retention) and Ana (Compliance) join reviews.

## Sprint cadence

- Two-week sprints, starting on Monday
- **Sprint planning:** Monday 10:00, 1 h
- **Daily standup:** 09:30, 15 min
- **Backlog refinement:** Wednesday 14:00, 45 min. Items must meet the Definition of Ready to leave refinement
- **Sprint review** with Retention and Compliance: last Friday 15:00
- **Retrospective:** last Friday 16:00

## Tools

- **GitHub Project "Tulip Churn Board"** for backlog and sprints. Columns: Backlog → Ready → In progress → In review → Done
- **GitHub issues** for epics, features, PBIs and bugs (issue types + sub-issues)
- **Notion** (this space) for documentation, decisions and meeting notes
- **AI coding assistants** (Claude Code, Codex) connected to GitHub and Notion. Conventions for them are in `CLAUDE.md` in the repo

## Conventions

- Branches: `feat/<issue>-slug`, `fix/<issue>-slug`
- Commits: Conventional Commits
- Every PR references its issue and follows the Definition of Done
